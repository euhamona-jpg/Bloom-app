Mental Health A–Z 📖

Short, simple information — not a diagnosis.

AnxietyAnxiety can make thoughts race and the body feel on alert. Small grounding steps, rest and talking to someone can help. If it is persistent or overwhelming, professional support may help.
DepressionDepression can affect mood, energy, sleep, concentration and hope. It is more than simply having a bad day. Support from a healthcare professional can be important.
LonelinessYou can feel lonely even when other people are around. One small connection — a message, a walk with someone, or asking for company — can be a beginning.
Burnout & stressLong-lasting pressure can leave you mentally and physically exhausted. Rest, boundaries, support and practical changes matter.
