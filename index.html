Mental Health A–Z 📖

Short, simple explanations. Learning about mental health can make the next step easier.

Anxiety

Anxiety can make your body and thoughts feel constantly alert. It can include worry, tension, racing thoughts or physical symptoms.

Depression

Depression is more than simply feeling sad. It can affect mood, energy, sleep, motivation and interest in things you normally care about.

Loneliness

You can feel lonely even when other people are around. Connection, one small contact at a time, can sometimes help.

Burnout

Long-term stress can leave you emotionally and physically exhausted. Rest, support and reducing the load can be important steps.

My child needs help 👨‍👩‍👧

You don't have to know exactly what to do. Start with the next small step.

My child doesn't want to go to school

Listen first. Ask what feels hardest, without immediately blaming or forcing an explanation.

I'm worried about bullying

Take the concern seriously. Keep notes of what is happening and involve the school and appropriate professionals when needed.

My child says they don't want to live

Take this seriously. Stay with the child, seek immediate professional help, and use emergency services if there is immediate danger.

I'm glad you told me. ❤️

If you are thinking about hurting yourself or you may be in immediate danger, please don't stay alone.

Right now

Go to a trusted person and tell them clearly that you need help. Move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself.

If you have already hurt yourself, taken an overdose, or believe you may act now, contact your local emergency service immediately.
